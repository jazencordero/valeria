# Decisions

Newest on top. Format: date, decision, why.

## 2026-11-14: v0 stores data in the browser only
Fastest way to get it in front of the first user. Accounts and sync wait until she uses it regularly.

## 2026-11-14: Each exam assumes all weekly study hours
Keeps v0 simple. Optimistic when exams overlap. Revisit when a user has 2+ exams close together.

## 2026-11-14: "What next" picks by urgency
Urgency = hours left on a topic / days until its exam, with a 2.5x boost for topics already in progress. Short sessions work best when she continues warm context instead of opening something new. (Started at 1.25x; it skipped her in-progress topic in testing.)

## 2026-11-14: Focus on readiness + fast start, not study materials
StudyFetch and others already generate flashcards and quizzes. Our edge is answering "will I be ready?" and removing start-up friction.
