# Product

## Problem

The first user is a student who also works and does extracurriculars. Her study time comes in short, broken pieces.

1. **She can't see at a glance whether she'll be ready.** How long each concept needs, whether questions are prepared, and whether the time left is enough.
2. **Every session starts with wasted minutes** rebuilding context: where was I, what's next.

## The job

> "In the next 30 minutes I have, what's the most valuable thing to study, and how do I start immediately?"

## Who it's for

Busy working students. Start with one real user, then her classmates.

## Principles

- Readiness is measured by being able to answer questions, not by hours spent.
- Getting started must take under 10 seconds.
- Calm and pleasant to open. If it feels like a chore, it won't get opened.
- Do one job well instead of being a study OS.

## Positioning

StudyFetch, Quizlet and NotebookLM help students **make study materials**. Ready tells busy students **whether they'll be ready, and gets them studying right away.**

## Success signals (v0)

- She opens it at least 3 times a week without being reminded.
- From opening the app to studying takes under 10 seconds.
- She says the on-track number matches how she actually feels.
